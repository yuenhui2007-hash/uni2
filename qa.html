ACW1120 – Financial Accounting 1

Objective (MCQ)

Easy The accounting equation is:
A. Assets = Liabilities − Equity
B. Assets = Liabilities + Equity
C. Assets + Liabilities = Equity
D. Assets + Equity = Liabilities
Answer: B — Assets = Liabilities + Equity is the fundamental equation.
Easy Which account is debited when rent is paid in advance for 12 months?
A. Rent Expense
B. Prepaid Rent
C. Cash
D. Unearned Revenue
Answer: B — Payment in advance creates a prepaid asset. Cash is credited.
Easy Normal balance of a liability account is:
A. Debit
B. Credit
C. Either, depending on size
D. Zero
Answer: B — Liabilities increase with credits; normal balance is credit.
Medium The two fundamental qualitative characteristics are:
A. Comparability and Verifiability
B. Relevance and Faithful Representation
C. Timeliness and Understandability
D. Neutrality and Materiality
Answer: B — The others are enhancing characteristics (or aspects of the fundamentals).
Medium Under the accrual basis, revenue is recognised when:
A. Cash is received from the customer
B. The invoice is printed
C. It is earned, regardless of when cash is received
D. The financial year ends
Answer: C
Medium Adjusting entries never involve which account?
A. Prepaid Rent
B. Depreciation Expense
C. Cash
D. Unearned Revenue
Answer: C — Adjusting entries allocate amounts already recorded; they do not involve cash.
Hard Accumulated Depreciation is best classified as:
A. An expense account
B. A liability account
C. A contra-asset account
D. An equity account
Answer: C — It offsets the asset; carrying amount = Cost − Accumulated Depreciation.
Hard Materiality is best described as an aspect of:
A. Faithful representation only
B. Relevance
C. Verifiability
D. Comparability
Answer: B — Materiality is an entity-specific aspect of relevance.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy Assets RM120,000; Liabilities RM45,000. Calculate equity.
Equity = Assets − Liabilities = 120,000 − 45,000 = RM75,000
Easy Define an asset according to the Conceptual Framework.
A present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events. An economic resource is a right that has the potential to produce economic benefits.
Medium Equipment cost RM48,000; residual RM6,000; useful life 7 years. Calculate annual straight-line depreciation and the year-end adjusting entry.
Depreciable amount = 42,000; Annual dep. = RM6,000
Dr Depreciation Expense 6,000
Cr Accumulated Depreciation – Equipment 6,000
Medium On 1 Oct received RM9,000 for 6 months’ service. Year-end 31 Dec. What adjusting entry is needed?
3 months earned → 9,000 × 3/6 = RM4,500
Dr Unearned Revenue 4,500
Cr Service Revenue 4,500
Hard Explain cash basis vs accrual basis. Why does the Conceptual Framework prefer accrual for general-purpose financial statements?
Cash: recognise only when cash is received/paid.
Accrual: recognise when earned/incurred.
Framework prefers accrual because it better reflects performance and position by matching revenues with related expenses and recognising assets/liabilities when rights and obligations arise — more useful for users’ decisions.
Easy A business has capital RM50,000 and liabilities RM20,000. What are total assets?
Assets = Liabilities + Equity = 20,000 + 50,000 = RM70,000
Easy State the debit/credit rule for expenses and for revenues.
Expenses: Debit to increase, Credit to decrease.
Revenues: Credit to increase, Debit to decrease.
Medium Salaries earned by staff RM3,500 have not been paid or recorded at year-end. Prepare the adjusting entry.
Dr Salaries Expense 3,500
Cr Salaries Payable 3,500
(Accrued expense)
Medium Prepaid insurance RM6,000 covers 12 months from 1 July. Year-end is 31 December. What is the adjusting entry?
6 months expired → 6,000 × 6/12 = RM3,000
Dr Insurance Expense 3,000
Cr Prepaid Insurance 3,000
Hard Machine cost RM90,000 on 1 Jan; residual RM10,000; useful life 8 years. After 3 years, what is the carrying amount? Show workings.
Annual dep. = (90,000 − 10,000)/8 = 10,000
Accum. dep. after 3 years = 30,000
Carrying amount = 90,000 − 30,000 = RM60,000
Hard Distinguish relevance and faithful representation. Give one example of a trade-off between them.
Relevance: information can influence decisions (predictive/confirmatory value).
Faithful representation: complete, neutral, free from error as far as possible.
Trade-off example: a timely estimate may be highly relevant but less precise than a delayed exact figure.

BFW1001 – Foundations of Finance

Objective (MCQ)

Easy Time value of money states that:
A. Money loses value only because of inflation
B. A ringgit today is worth more than a ringgit in the future
C. Future cash flows are always worth more than present ones
D. Interest rates do not affect value
Answer: B
Easy NPV decision rule for an independent project is:
A. Accept if NPV < 0
B. Accept if NPV > 0
C. Accept if NPV = 0 only
D. Accept if payback is longer
Answer: B
Medium Which method directly measures expected increase in firm value?
A. Payback period
B. Accounting rate of return
C. Net Present Value (NPV)
D. Profitability index only
Answer: C
Medium Ordinary annuity means payments occur:
A. At the beginning of each period
B. At the end of each period
C. Only once at maturity
D. Randomly within the year
Answer: B
Hard Unsystematic risk can be reduced mainly by:
A. Investing only in government bonds
B. Diversification across many assets
C. Increasing financial leverage
D. Holding a single stock long term
Answer: B
Hard A limitation of the IRR method is:
A. It is expressed as a percentage
B. Multiple IRRs can occur with non-conventional cash flows
C. It always agrees with NPV ranking
D. It ignores the time value of money
Answer: B

Subjective / Calculation

Easy Calculate FV of RM12,000 at 8% p.a. for 5 years (annual compounding).
FV = 12,000 × (1.08)5 = RM17,632
Easy What is the present value of RM25,000 to be received in 4 years if r = 9%?
PV = 25,000 / (1.09)4 ≈ RM17,710
Medium Project cost RM80,000; CF: Y1 30k, Y2 35k, Y3 40k; r = 10%. Calculate NPV and decide.
PV inflows ≈ 86,252; NPV = +RM6,252 → Accept
Hard Explain the risk–return trade-off and why managers should use higher discount rates for riskier projects.
Investors require higher expected returns for higher risk. The discount rate is the opportunity cost of capital for that risk. Using too low a rate overstates NPV and may accept value-destroying projects.
Easy What is the future value of RM5,000 invested for 3 years at 6% p.a. compounded annually?
FV = 5,000 × (1.06)3 = 5,000 × 1.191016 ≈ RM5,955
Easy State the NPV decision rule for independent projects.
Accept if NPV > 0; reject if NPV < 0. NPV = 0 means the project earns exactly the required return.
Medium Calculate the PV of an ordinary annuity of RM4,000 per year for 5 years at 9%.
PVA = 4,000 × [1 − (1.09)−5]/0.09 ≈ 4,000 × 3.8897 ≈ RM15,559
Medium A project costs RM50,000. Cash flows: Y1 18,000; Y2 22,000; Y3 25,000. Required return 10%. Calculate NPV.
PV = 16,364 + 18,182 + 18,783 = 53,329
NPV = 53,329 − 50,000 = +RM3,329 → Accept
Hard Why is NPV generally preferred to IRR when ranking mutually exclusive projects?
NPV measures value added in ringgit and assumes reinvestment at the cost of capital. IRR can give conflicting rankings when projects differ in size or cash-flow timing, and may have multiple rates. Maximising NPV is consistent with maximising shareholder wealth.
Hard Distinguish systematic and unsystematic risk. Which is compensated in a well-diversified portfolio?
Unsystematic: firm-specific; can be diversified away.
Systematic: market-wide; cannot be diversified away.
Only systematic risk is compensated with higher expected return.

BTW1042 – Business Law

Objective (MCQ)

Easy A price tag on goods in a self-service store is generally:
A. An offer
B. An invitation to treat
C. An acceptance
D. A binding contract
Answer: B
Easy Which is an essential element of a valid contract?
A. Written form in all cases
B. Offer and acceptance
C. Registration with Companies Commission
D. Payment by bank transfer only
Answer: B
Medium Which is NOT generally essential for a valid contract under Malaysian law?
A. Offer and acceptance
B. Consideration
C. Written form
D. Intention to create legal relations
Answer: C — Most contracts need not be in writing.
Medium A conditional acceptance that adds new terms is treated as:
A. Valid acceptance
B. A counter-offer
C. An invitation to treat
D. Past consideration
Answer: B — It rejects the original offer.
Hard A private limited company (Sdn Bhd) under the Companies Act 2016 has:
A. No separate legal personality; unlimited liability
B. Separate legal entity; shareholders’ liability limited to unpaid shares
C. Same legal status as a partnership
D. Owner and business as the same legal person
Answer: B

Subjective

Easy List the four elements required to establish the tort of negligence.
1. Duty of care 2. Breach of duty 3. Causation 4. Damage not too remote
Medium Using IRAC: Ali offers to sell his car to Ben for RM40,000. Ben replies “I accept, but only if you include the spare tyre.” Ali remains silent. Is there a contract?
Issue: Valid acceptance?
Rule: Acceptance must be absolute; new terms = counter-offer that rejects the original offer.
Application: Ben’s reply is a counter-offer; silence ≠ acceptance.
Conclusion: No contract.
Hard Compare sole proprietorship, partnership and Sdn Bhd on legal personality and liability.
Sole prop: no separate personality; unlimited liability.
Partnership: generally no separate personality; unlimited joint & several liability.
Sdn Bhd: separate legal entity; limited liability.
Easy What is consideration in contract law?
Something of value in the eyes of the law given in exchange for a promise. It must be sufficient but need not be adequate. Past consideration is generally not valid.
Easy Name two sources of written law in Malaysia.
Federal Constitution; Acts of Parliament (also state enactments and subsidiary legislation).
Medium Explain the difference between an offer and an invitation to treat, with one example of each.
Offer: definite proposal intended to become binding on acceptance (e.g. “I will sell you this car for RM40,000”).
Invitation to treat: willingness to negotiate (e.g. goods on supermarket shelves, most advertisements).
Medium Briefly explain actual authority and apparent authority in agency.
Actual authority: express or implied power given by the principal to the agent.
Apparent authority: arises when the principal’s representation leads a third party to reasonably believe the agent has authority (estoppel).
Hard A professional accountant gives negligent tax advice to a client who relies on it and incurs penalties. Outline how the four elements of negligence could be argued.
Duty: accountant–client relationship creates a duty of care.
Breach: advice fell below the standard of a reasonably competent accountant.
Causation: but for the advice, the client would not have suffered the loss.
Remoteness: financial loss of this type was reasonably foreseeable.

ECM1953 – Principles of Economics

Objective (MCQ)

Easy If demand increases and supply is constant, equilibrium price and quantity will:
A. Both fall
B. Both rise
C. Price rises, quantity falls
D. Price falls, quantity rises
Answer: B
Easy Opportunity cost is:
A. The accounting cost of a resource
B. The value of the next best alternative forgone
C. Always equal to fixed cost
D. Zero when resources are free in money terms
Answer: B
Medium A firm maximises profit where:
A. Price = Average cost
B. Marginal revenue = Marginal cost
C. Total revenue is maximised
D. Average variable cost is minimised
Answer: B
Hard If |PED| < 1 and the firm raises price, total revenue will:
A. Fall
B. Rise
C. Stay the same
D. Become zero
Answer: B — Demand is inelastic.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy Price rises from RM10 to RM12; quantity demanded falls from 200 to 160. Calculate PED.
%ΔP = 20%; %ΔQd = −20%; PED = −1 → unit elastic
Medium Fixed costs RM20,000; VC per unit RM15; SP RM25. Break-even quantity?
CM = 10; BE = 20,000 / 10 = 2,000 units
Hard Using AD–AS logic, explain the short-run effect of an increase in government spending on output and the price level.
G ↑ → AD shifts right → higher equilibrium real output and higher price level in the short run (movement along SRAS).
Easy State the law of demand and the law of supply.
Demand: other things equal, higher price → lower quantity demanded.
Supply: other things equal, higher price → higher quantity supplied.
Easy What is the difference between a movement along a demand curve and a shift of the demand curve?
Movement along: caused by a change in the good’s own price.
Shift: caused by other factors (income, tastes, prices of related goods, etc.).
Medium Price falls from RM20 to RM16; quantity demanded rises from 100 to 130. Calculate PED and state whether demand is elastic or inelastic.
%ΔP = −20%; %ΔQd = +30%; PED = 30/−20 = −1.5 → elastic (|PED| > 1)
Medium Compare perfect competition and monopoly in terms of number of firms and price-setting power.
Perfect competition: many firms, identical product, price taker.
Monopoly: one firm, unique product, price maker (subject to demand).
Hard Explain how expansionary fiscal policy is expected to affect AD, output and the price level in the short run.
Higher G or lower taxes raise AD (shift right). Short-run equilibrium moves to higher real output and a higher price level along the SRAS curve.

Objective (MCQ)

Easy Which measure of central tendency is least affected by extreme outliers?
A. Mean
B. Median
C. Mode only when multimodal
D. Range
Answer: B
Medium A Type I error occurs when:
A. We fail to reject a false null hypothesis
B. We reject a true null hypothesis
C. The sample size is too small
D. The p-value is exactly 0.05
Answer: B — False positive.
Hard In Ŷ = 15 + 3.2X (X = advertising RM’000), the slope 3.2 means:
A. Sales are always RM3,200
B. Each extra RM1,000 of advertising is associated with about RM3,200 higher sales on average
C. Advertising is 3.2% of sales
D. The intercept is irrelevant
Answer: B

Subjective / Calculation

Easy Data: 5, 7, 9, 11, 13. Calculate the mean.
Mean = 9
Medium Same data. Calculate the sample standard deviation.
Sample variance = 10; s = √10 ≈ 3.16
Hard Explain the difference between a population parameter and a sample statistic. Why do we usually work with statistics?
Parameter = characteristic of the whole population (e.g. μ). Statistic = from a sample (e.g. x̄). Full populations are often impractical to measure; we infer about parameters using samples.
Easy Data: 4, 8, 8, 10, 15. Find the median and the mode.
Ordered already. Median = 8; Mode = 8
Easy What does a 95% confidence interval for a mean conceptually mean?
If the sampling process were repeated many times, about 95% of the intervals constructed would contain the true population mean.
Medium Data: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. Calculate the sample variance and sample standard deviation.
Mean = 6; squared deviations sum = 40; s² = 40/4 = 10; s = √10 ≈ 3.16
Medium What is a Type II error in hypothesis testing?
Failing to reject a false null hypothesis (false negative).
Hard A regression gives Ŷ = 20 + 1.5X with R² = 0.64. Interpret the slope and R² in words.
Slope 1.5: a one-unit increase in X is associated with a 1.5-unit increase in predicted Y on average.
R² 0.64: 64% of the variation in Y is explained by X in this sample.

MGW1010 – Introduction to Management

Objective (MCQ)

Easy Managers who believe employees are self-motivated follow:
A. Theory X
B. Theory Y
C. Scientific Management only
D. Bureaucracy
Answer: B (McGregor)
Easy The four functions of management are:
A. Plan, Budget, Audit, Report
B. Planning, Organising, Leading, Controlling
C. Hire, Train, Motivate, Fire
D. Strategy, Tactics, Operations, Logistics
Answer: B
Medium The contingency approach argues that:
A. There is always one best way to manage
B. The best approach depends on the situation
C. Only classical theory is valid
D. Structure never matters
Answer: B
Hard Transformational leadership primarily:
A. Focuses only on contingent rewards
B. Inspires followers to transcend self-interest via vision and stimulation
C. Avoids any exchange with followers
D. Is identical to laissez-faire leadership
Answer: B

Subjective

Easy Name the four functions of management.
Planning, Organising, Leading, Controlling
Medium Distinguish transactional and transformational leadership with a brief example of each.
Transactional: exchange-based (e.g. commission sales manager).
Transformational: inspires via vision (e.g. CEO driving a sustainability mission).
Hard Critically evaluate Scientific Management in modern knowledge work or remote teams.
Strengths: efficiency, measurement, standardisation. Weaknesses: mechanistic view of workers, ignores social needs, assumes one best way. Poor fit for creative/non-routine remote work — blend with human-relations and contingency thinking.
Easy Define effectiveness and efficiency in management.
Effectiveness: doing the right things (achieving goals).
Efficiency: doing things right (minimising resource waste).
Easy What does Theory X assume about workers (McGregor)?
Workers dislike work, avoid responsibility, and need to be controlled and directed.
Medium Summarise Herzberg’s two-factor theory.
Hygiene factors (pay, conditions, policy) prevent dissatisfaction but do not strongly motivate.
Motivators (achievement, recognition, work itself, responsibility, growth) drive satisfaction and motivation.
Medium What is the main idea of Weber’s bureaucracy, and name one criticism.
Ideal type: hierarchy, formal rules, impersonality, merit-based advancement.
Criticism: can become rigid, slow, and rule-bound (red tape / goal displacement).
Hard Explain the contingency approach and why it challenges classical “one best way” thinking.
Contingency theory argues that effective management depends on situational factors (environment, technology, size, people). There is no universal best method; practices that work in one context may fail in another.

ACW2220 – Management Accounting 1

Objective (MCQ)

Easy Contribution margin equals:
A. Sales − Fixed costs
B. Sales − All variable costs
C. Sales − Cost of goods sold only
D. Profit + Fixed costs
Answer: B
Easy Fixed costs in total (within the relevant range):
A. Rise in proportion to activity
B. Stay constant
C. Always equal variable costs
D. Are always irrelevant
Answer: B
Medium In a special-order decision with spare capacity, relevant costs are:
A. All fixed costs allocated to the product
B. Sunk costs of existing equipment
C. Incremental variable costs and any additional fixed costs caused by the order
D. Historical full absorption cost only
Answer: C
Hard When deciding make-or-buy, allocated fixed overhead that continues either way should be:
A. Included in the cost to make
B. Treated as a relevant cost savings if buying
C. Ignored (irrelevant)
D. Doubled for safety
Answer: C — It does not differ between alternatives.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy SP RM90, VC RM54, FC RM108,000. Calculate CM per unit and break-even units.
CM = 36; BE = 108,000 / 36 = 3,000 units
Medium Spare capacity. Customer offers RM70 for 800 units. Normal SP RM95, VC RM55, FC unchanged. Accept?
Incremental profit = (70 − 55) × 800 = RM12,000 → Accept
Medium Units needed to earn a target profit of RM36,000 (same data: CM 36, FC 108,000)?
(108,000 + 36,000) / 36 = 4,000 units
Hard Make cost: DM 18 + DL 12 + Var OH 7 + Allocated Fixed OH 9. Supplier quote RM35. Fixed OH continues. Advise make or buy.
Relevant make cost = 18+12+7 = RM37 (ignore continuing allocated fixed).
Buy = RM35 → Buy (save RM2/unit) if quality/reliability OK.
Easy SP RM50, VC RM30, FC RM40,000. Calculate CM per unit and break-even units.
CM = 20; BE = 40,000 / 20 = 2,000 units
Easy What is a sunk cost? Are sunk costs relevant to future decisions?
A cost already incurred that cannot be changed. Not relevant to forward-looking decisions.
Medium SP RM80, VC RM50, FC RM90,000. How many units are needed for a target profit of RM30,000?
CM = 30; Units = (90,000 + 30,000) / 30 = 4,000 units
Medium Define margin of safety and state why managers care about it.
Margin of safety = Actual (or budgeted) sales − Break-even sales. It shows how far sales can fall before a loss — a measure of risk/cushion.
Hard A segment has CM RM80,000 and avoidable fixed costs RM50,000. Allocated common fixed costs RM40,000 would remain if dropped. Should the segment be kept or dropped? Explain.
If dropped: lose CM 80,000, save only 50,000 → net loss of 30,000 contribution.
Common fixed 40,000 is irrelevant (continues). Keep the segment.
Hard Special order: price RM60, VC RM45, extra fixed costs RM2,000, quantity 500, spare capacity. Calculate incremental profit and recommend.
Incremental revenue = 30,000; incremental VC = 22,500; extra FC = 2,000
Incremental profit = 30,000 − 22,500 − 2,000 = RM5,500 → Accept
