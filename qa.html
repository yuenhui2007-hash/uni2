ACW1120 – Financial Accounting 1

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   The accounting equation is:
A. Assets = Liabilities − Equity
B. Assets = Liabilities + Equity
C. Assets + Liabilities = Equity
D. Assets + Equity = Liabilities

Answer: B — Assets = Liabilities + Equity is the fundamental accounting equation.

Easy   Which account is debited when rent is paid in advance for 12 months?
A. Rent Expense
B. Prepaid Rent
C. Cash
D. Unearned Revenue

Answer: B — Payment in advance creates a prepaid asset (Prepaid Rent). Cash is credited.

Medium   Which pair are the two fundamental qualitative characteristics under the Conceptual Framework?
A. Comparability and Verifiability
B. Relevance and Faithful Representation
C. Timeliness and Understandability
D. Neutrality and Materiality

Answer: B — Relevance and Faithful Representation are fundamental; the others are enhancing (or aspects of them).

Medium   Under the accrual basis, revenue is recognised when:
A. Cash is received from the customer
B. The invoice is printed
C. It is earned (performance obligation satisfied), regardless of cash timing
D. The financial year ends

Answer: C — Accrual accounting recognises revenue when earned, not when cash is collected.

Hard   Accumulated Depreciation is best classified as:
A. An expense account
B. A liability account
C. A contra-asset account
D. An equity account

Answer: C — It offsets the related asset; carrying amount = Cost − Accumulated Depreciation.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   Assets RM120,000; Liabilities RM45,000. Calculate equity.
Equity = Assets − Liabilities = 120,000 − 45,000 = RM75,000
Medium   Equipment cost RM48,000; residual RM6,000; useful life 7 years. Calculate annual straight-line depreciation and write the year-end adjusting entry.
Depreciable amount = 48,000 − 6,000 = 42,000
Annual depreciation = 42,000 ÷ 7 = RM6,000
Dr Depreciation Expense 6,000
   Cr Accumulated Depreciation – Equipment 6,000
Hard   Explain the difference between the cash basis and the accrual basis of accounting. Why does the Conceptual Framework prefer the accrual basis for general-purpose financial statements?
Cash basis: recognise revenues/expenses only when cash is received or paid.
Accrual basis: recognise revenues when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of cash timing.

The Framework prefers accrual because it better reflects performance and position by matching revenues with related expenses and by recognising assets and liabilities when rights and obligations arise, giving users more relevant and faithfully representative information for decision-making.

BFW1001 – Foundations of Finance

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   The time value of money concept states that:
A. Money loses value only because of inflation
B. A ringgit today is worth more than a ringgit in the future
C. Future cash flows are always worth more than present ones
D. Interest rates do not affect value

Answer: B — Because of earning potential, inflation and risk.

Medium   Which capital budgeting method directly measures the expected increase in firm value?
A. Payback period
B. Accounting rate of return
C. Net Present Value (NPV)
D. Profitability index only

Answer: C — NPV is the present value of net cash flows and equals expected value created.

Hard   Unsystematic risk can be reduced mainly by:
A. Investing only in government bonds
B. Diversification across many assets
C. Increasing financial leverage
D. Holding a single stock long term

Answer: B — Firm-specific risk averages out in a diversified portfolio; systematic risk remains.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   Calculate FV of RM12,000 invested for 5 years at 8% p.a. compounded annually.
FV = 12,000 × (1.08)5 = 12,000 × 1.4693 = RM17,632
Medium   Project costs RM80,000. Cash inflows: Y1 30,000; Y2 35,000; Y3 40,000. r = 10%. Calculate NPV and decide.
PV = 30k/1.1 + 35k/1.21 + 40k/1.331 ≈ 27,273 + 28,926 + 30,053 = 86,252
NPV = 86,252 − 80,000 = +RM6,252 → Accept
Hard   Explain the risk–return trade-off and why managers should use higher discount rates for riskier projects.
Investors require higher expected returns to compensate for higher risk. The discount rate is the opportunity cost of capital for a project of given risk. Using too low a rate for a risky project overstates NPV and may lead to accepting value-destroying projects; using too high a rate may reject good projects. Matching rate to risk protects shareholder value.

BTW1042 – Business Law

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   A price tag on goods in a self-service store is generally:
A. An offer
B. An invitation to treat
C. An acceptance
D. A binding contract

Answer: B — The customer makes the offer at the cashier; the store accepts by taking payment.

Medium   Which is NOT generally an essential element of a valid contract under Malaysian law?
A. Offer and acceptance
B. Consideration
C. Written form
D. Intention to create legal relations

Answer: C — Most contracts need not be in writing (exceptions exist, e.g. land).

Hard   Which best describes a private limited company (Sdn Bhd) under the Companies Act 2016?
A. No separate legal personality; unlimited liability
B. Separate legal entity; shareholders’ liability limited to unpaid shares
C. Same as a partnership under the Partnership Act 1961
D. Owner and business are the same legal person

Answer: B

Subjective

Easy   List the four elements required to establish the tort of negligence.
1. Duty of care
2. Breach of duty
3. Causation
4. Damage not too remote (reasonably foreseeable type of loss)
Medium   Using IRAC: Ali offers to sell his car to Ben for RM40,000. Ben replies “I accept, but only if you include the spare tyre.” Ali stays silent. Is there a contract?
Issue: Valid acceptance?
Rule: Acceptance must be absolute and unqualified; a conditional acceptance is a counter-offer that rejects the original offer.
Application: Ben introduced a new term → counter-offer. Silence ≠ acceptance of the counter-offer.
Conclusion: No contract.
Hard   Compare sole proprietorship, partnership and Sdn Bhd on legal personality and liability.
Sole prop: no separate personality; unlimited liability.
Partnership: generally no separate personality; unlimited joint & several liability.
Sdn Bhd: separate legal entity (Companies Act 2016); limited liability to amount unpaid on shares.

ECM1953 – Principles of Economics

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   If demand increases and supply is unchanged, equilibrium price and quantity will:
A. Both fall
B. Both rise
C. Price rises, quantity falls
D. Price falls, quantity rises

Answer: B

Medium   A firm maximises profit in the short run by producing where:
A. Price = Average cost
B. Marginal revenue = Marginal cost
C. Total revenue is maximised
D. Average variable cost is minimised

Answer: B

Hard   If |PED| < 1 and the firm raises price, total revenue will:
A. Fall
B. Rise
C. Stay the same
D. Become zero

Answer: B — Inelastic demand: % fall in Qd is smaller than % rise in P, so revenue rises.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   Price rises from RM10 to RM12; Qd falls from 200 to 160. Calculate PED.
%ΔP = 20%; %ΔQd = −20%; PED = −1 → unit elastic
Medium   Fixed costs RM20,000; VC per unit RM15; SP RM25. Calculate break-even quantity.
CM = 10; BE = 20,000 / 10 = 2,000 units
Hard   Using AD–AS logic, explain the short-run effect of an increase in government spending on output and the price level.
G ↑ raises AD (AD shifts right). In the short run the economy moves along SRAS to higher real output and a higher price level. Magnitude depends on the multiplier and how close the economy is to capacity.

ETW1001 – Statistical Analysis

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   Which measure of central tendency is least affected by extreme outliers?
A. Mean
B. Median
C. Mode only when multimodal
D. Range

Answer: B

Medium   A Type I error occurs when:
A. We fail to reject a false null hypothesis
B. We reject a true null hypothesis
C. The sample size is too small
D. The p-value is exactly 0.05

Answer: B — False positive.

Hard   In Ŷ = 15 + 3.2X (X = advertising RM’000, Y = sales RM’000), the slope 3.2 means:
A. Sales are always RM3,200
B. Each extra RM1,000 of advertising is associated with RM3,200 higher sales on average
C. Advertising is 3.2% of sales
D. Intercept is irrelevant

Answer: B

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   Data: 5, 7, 9, 11, 13. Calculate the mean.
Mean = (5+7+9+11+13)/5 = 9
Medium   Same data. Calculate the sample standard deviation.
Deviations from 9: −4,−2,0,2,4; squared sum = 40
Sample variance = 40/(5−1) = 10; s = √10 ≈ 3.16
Hard   Explain the difference between a population parameter and a sample statistic. Why do we usually work with statistics?
Parameter = numerical characteristic of the whole population (e.g. μ). Statistic = calculated from a sample (e.g. x̄). We use samples because measuring the entire population is often impractical; we then infer about parameters using confidence intervals and hypothesis tests.

MGW1010 – Introduction to Management

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   Managers who believe employees are self-motivated and can accept responsibility follow:
A. Theory X
B. Theory Y
C. Scientific Management only
D. Bureaucracy

Answer: B (McGregor)

Medium   The contingency approach argues that:
A. There is always one best way to manage
B. The best approach depends on the situation
C. Only classical theory is valid
D. Structure never matters

Answer: B

Hard   Transformational leadership primarily:
A. Focuses only on contingent rewards
B. Inspires followers to transcend self-interest via vision and stimulation
C. Avoids any exchange with followers
D. Is identical to laissez-faire leadership

Answer: B

Subjective

Easy   Name the four functions of management.
Planning, Organising, Leading, Controlling
Medium   Distinguish transactional and transformational leadership with a brief example of each.
Transactional: exchange-based (rewards for performance). Example: commission-only sales manager.
Transformational: inspires via vision, intellectual stimulation, individualised consideration. Example: CEO mobilising staff around a sustainability vision.
Hard   Critically evaluate Scientific Management in the context of modern knowledge work or remote teams.
Strengths: efficiency, measurement, standardisation. Weaknesses: mechanistic view of workers, ignores social/psychological needs, assumes one best way. In knowledge work and remote settings, autonomy, creativity and intrinsic motivation matter more; pure Taylorism often demotivates and fits poorly with non-routine tasks. Modern practice blends efficiency ideas with human-relations and contingency thinking.

ACW2220 – Management Accounting 1

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   Contribution margin equals:
A. Sales − Fixed costs
B. Sales − All variable costs
C. Sales − Cost of goods sold only
D. Profit + Fixed costs

Answer: B

Medium   In a special-order decision with spare capacity, which costs are relevant?
A. All fixed costs allocated to the product
B. Sunk costs of existing equipment
C. Incremental variable costs and any additional fixed costs caused by the order
D. Historical full absorption cost only

Answer: C

Hard   When deciding make-or-buy, allocated fixed overhead that will continue either way should be:
A. Included in the cost to make
B. Treated as a relevant cost savings if buying
C. Ignored (irrelevant)
D. Doubled for safety

Answer: C — It does not differ between alternatives.

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   SP RM90, VC RM54, FC RM108,000. Calculate CM per unit and break-even units.
CM = 36; BE units = 108,000 / 36 = 3,000 units
Medium   Spare capacity. Customer offers RM70 for 800 units. Normal SP RM95, VC RM55, FC unchanged. Accept?
Incremental profit = (70 − 55) × 800 = RM12,000 → Accept
Hard   Make cost: DM 18 + DL 12 + Var OH 7 + Allocated Fixed OH 9. Supplier quote RM35. Fixed OH continues. Advise make or buy and explain.
Relevant make cost = 18+12+7 = RM37 (ignore RM9 allocated fixed — continues either way).
Buy cost = RM35 → Buy (saves RM2/unit), assuming quality and supply reliability are acceptable.
