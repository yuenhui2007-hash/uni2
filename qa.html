ACW1120 – Financial Accounting 1

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   The accounting equation is:▼
A. Assets = Liabilities − Equity
B. Assets = Liabilities + Equity
C. Assets + Liabilities = Equity
D. Assets + Equity = Liabilities

Answer: B — Assets = Liabilities + Equity.

Easy   Which account is debited when rent is paid in advance for 12 months?▼
A. Rent Expense
B. Prepaid Rent
C. Cash
D. Unearned Revenue

Answer: B — Creates a prepaid asset.

Medium   The two fundamental qualitative characteristics are:▼
A. Comparability and Verifiability
B. Relevance and Faithful Representation
C. Timeliness and Understandability
D. Neutrality and Materiality

Answer: B

Medium   Under the accrual basis, revenue is recognised when:▼
A. Cash is received
B. The invoice is printed
C. It is earned, regardless of cash timing
D. The financial year ends

Answer: C

Hard   Accumulated Depreciation is best classified as:▼
A. An expense account
B. A liability account
C. A contra-asset account
D. An equity account

Answer: C

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   Assets RM120,000; Liabilities RM45,000. Calculate equity.▼
Equity = 120,000 − 45,000 = RM75,000
Medium   Equipment RM48,000; residual RM6,000; life 7 years. Annual straight-line dep. and adjusting entry?▼
Depreciable = 42,000; Annual = RM6,000
Dr Depreciation Expense 6,000   Cr Accum. Depreciation 6,000
Hard   Explain cash basis vs accrual basis. Why does the Framework prefer accrual?▼
Cash: recognise only when cash moves. Accrual: when earned/incurred. Framework prefers accrual because it better reflects performance and position for decision-making.

BFW1001 – Foundations of Finance

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   Time value of money states that:▼
A. Money loses value only due to inflation
B. A ringgit today is worth more than a ringgit in the future
C. Future cash is always worth more
D. Interest rates do not affect value

Answer: B

Medium   Which method directly measures expected increase in firm value?▼
A. Payback period
B. Accounting rate of return
C. Net Present Value (NPV)
D. Profitability index only

Answer: C

Hard   Unsystematic risk is mainly reduced by:▼
A. Government bonds only
B. Diversification across many assets
C. Higher leverage
D. Holding one stock

Answer: B

Subjective / Calculation

Easy   FV of RM12,000 at 8% for 5 years (annual compounding)?▼
FV = 12,000 × (1.08)5 = RM17,632
Medium   Invest RM80,000; CF 30k, 35k, 40k; r=10%. NPV and decision?▼
PV ≈ 86,252; NPV = +RM6,252 → Accept
Hard   Explain the risk–return trade-off and why riskier projects need higher discount rates.▼
Investors require higher expected returns for higher risk. The discount rate is the opportunity cost of capital for that risk level; mismatching it distorts NPV and can destroy value.

BTW1042 – Business Law

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   A price tag in a self-service store is generally:▼
A. An offer
B. An invitation to treat
C. An acceptance
D. A binding contract

Answer: B

Medium   Which is NOT generally essential for a valid contract?▼
A. Offer and acceptance
B. Consideration
C. Written form
D. Intention to create legal relations

Answer: C

Hard   A Sdn Bhd under Companies Act 2016 has:▼
A. No separate personality; unlimited liability
B. Separate legal entity; limited liability
C. Same status as a partnership
D. Owner = business in law

Answer: B

Subjective

Easy   List the four elements of negligence.▼
Duty of care; Breach; Causation; Damage not too remote
Medium   IRAC: Ali offers car for RM40,000. Ben: “I accept if spare tyre included.” Ali silent. Contract?▼
Issue: Valid acceptance? Rule: Must be unqualified; new terms = counter-offer. Application: Ben’s reply is counter-offer; silence ≠ acceptance. Conclusion: No contract.
Hard   Compare sole prop, partnership and Sdn Bhd on personality and liability.▼
Sole prop: no separate personality, unlimited. Partnership: generally none, unlimited joint & several. Sdn Bhd: separate entity, limited liability.

MGW1010 – Introduction to Management

Objective (MCQ)

Easy   Belief that employees are self-motivated = ▼
A. Theory X
B. Theory Y
C. Scientific Management only
D. Bureaucracy

Answer: B

Medium   Contingency approach says:▼
A. One best way always
B. Best approach depends on the situation
C. Only classical theory works
D. Structure never matters

Answer: B

Hard   Transformational leadership primarily:▼
A. Uses only contingent rewards
B. Inspires followers to transcend self-interest
C. Avoids exchange with followers
D. Equals laissez-faire

Answer: B

Subjective

Easy   Name the four functions of management.▼
Planning, Organising, Leading, Controlling
Medium   Transactional vs transformational leadership + example each.▼
