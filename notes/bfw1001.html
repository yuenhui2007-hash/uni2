1. Time Value of Money

A ringgit today is worth more than a ringgit in the future because of earning potential, inflation and risk.

Timeline Concept

Core Formulas

ConceptFormula
Future Value (single sum)FV = PV × (1+r)n
Present Value (single sum)PV = FV / (1+r)n
PV of ordinary annuityPVA = PMT × [1−(1+r)−n] / r
FV of ordinary annuityFVA = PMT × [(1+r)n−1] / r
Net Present ValueNPV = −I₀ + Σ CFt/(1+r)t

2. Worked Examples

FV: RM12,000 at 8% for 5 years → 12,000 × (1.08)5 = RM17,632
PV: RM25,000 in 4 years, r=9% → 25,000 / (1.09)4 ≈ RM17,710
NPV: Invest RM80,000; CF: 30k, 35k, 40k; r=10%
PV inflows ≈ 27,273 + 28,926 + 30,053 = 86,252
NPV = 86,252 − 80,000 = +RM6,252 → Accept
