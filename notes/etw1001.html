ETW1001 – Introduction to Statistical Analysis

Advanced notes: estimator logic, interval interpretation, testing errors, regression limits

1. Statistical Reasoning in Business

Decisions under uncertainty require (i) summarising sample evidence and (ii) quantifying uncertainty about population parameters. Misinterpretation of p-values, confidence intervals and R² is more costly than arithmetic slips — exams increasingly test interpretation.

2. Descriptives with Design Intent

Mean vs median: use median under skewness/outliers; mean for roughly symmetric data and as input to many inferential procedures. Sample variance uses n−1 (Bessel’s correction) for unbiasedness of σ² under iid normal sampling. Empirical rule / Chebyshev: know which requires normality assumptions.

3. Estimation

Point estimator properties (informal): unbiasedness, efficiency, consistency. SE of the mean ≈ s/√n. CI for mean (σ known): x̄ ± zα/2 σ/√n; σ unknown: tn−1. Correct frequentist reading: the interval is random before data; the parameter is fixed; long-run coverage is 1−α. After observing data, avoid “probability the mean is in [a,b] is 0.95” unless Bayesian framework is taught.

4. Testing

H₀ as the straw-man of no effect / no difference; choose one- vs two-sided from the research claim before seeing data. p-value = probability, under H₀, of data as extreme or more extreme than observed — not P(H₀ true | data). Type I (α) vs Type II (β); power 1−β rises with n, effect size, α, and lower noise. Statistical significance ≠ economic significance (effect size, costs of error).

5. Simple Linear Regression

Y = β₀ + β₁X + ε  ;  Ŷ = b₀ + b₁X

OLS minimises Σe². b₁ is causal only under strong assumptions (random assignment / no omitted confounders / correct functional form). R² measures in-sample linear fit, not predictive validity out of sample and not causality. Residual plots diagnose nonlinearity and heteroscedasticity at the level required by the unit.

Exam: define hypotheses in symbols and words; interpret CI and slope in context units; refuse causal claims from observational regression without warrant.
