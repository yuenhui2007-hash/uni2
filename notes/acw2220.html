2. CVP Analysis

Contribution Margin (CM) = Sales − All Variable Costs
CM covers fixed costs first, then contributes to profit.
CM per unit = SP − VC per unit
Break-even (units) = Fixed Costs ÷ CM per unit
Break-even (RM) = Fixed Costs ÷ CM Ratio
Target-profit units = (FC + Target Profit) ÷ CM per unit

Break-even Chart

SP RM90, VC RM54, FC RM108,000, Target profit RM36,000
CM = 36 → BE units = 3,000 → BE sales = RM270,000
Target units = (108k + 36k) / 36 = 4,000 units

3. Relevant Costing Decisions

Relevant cost = future cost that differs between alternatives. Sunk costs and unchanged allocated fixed costs are irrelevant.
