ACW2220 – Management Accounting 1

Cost information for planning, control and decision-making inside the organisation.

1. Unit Overview

This unit focuses on the production and use of cost information to help managers plan, control operations and make decisions. Unlike financial accounting (external), management accounting is internal, forward-looking and not bound by accounting standards.

2. Key Definitions

Management Accounting
The process of identifying, measuring, analysing, interpreting and communicating information to managers to help achieve organisational objectives.
Cost Object
Anything for which a separate measurement of cost is desired (product, service, department, customer, project).
Direct Cost vs Indirect Cost
Direct – can be traced conveniently and economically to a cost object.
Indirect – cannot be traced conveniently; must be allocated.
Variable Cost vs Fixed Cost
Variable – changes in total in proportion to changes in activity level.
Fixed – remains constant in total within the relevant range.
Contribution Margin
Sales revenue minus all variable costs. It contributes towards covering fixed costs and then to profit.
Relevant Cost
A future cost that differs between alternatives. Sunk costs are irrelevant.

3. Core Concepts & Formulas

Cost Behaviour

Total Cost = Fixed Cost + (Variable Cost per unit × Activity level)

Cost-Volume-Profit (CVP) Analysis

Contribution Margin (CM) = Sales – Variable Costs
CM Ratio = CM / Sales
Break-even point (units) = Fixed Costs / CM per unit
Break-even point (RM) = Fixed Costs / CM Ratio
Target Profit units = (Fixed Costs + Target Profit) / CM per unit

Product Costing

  • Job costing – costs accumulated by individual job/order (custom work).
  • Process costing – costs averaged over identical/similar units (mass production).
  • Activity-Based Costing (ABC) – allocates overhead based on activities that drive costs.

Relevant Costing for Decisions

  • Special order decisions
  • Make or buy decisions
  • Keep or drop a product line
  • Product mix with constrained resources

Rule: Focus on differential (incremental) revenues and costs. Ignore sunk costs and allocated fixed costs that do not change.

4. Worked Examples

Break-even Calculation
Selling price = RM50; Variable cost = RM30; Fixed costs = RM40,000
CM per unit = 50 – 30 = RM20
Break-even units = 40,000 / 20 = 2,000 units
Break-even sales = 2,000 × 50 = RM100,000
Special Order
Normal price RM80; Variable cost RM45; Fixed costs unchanged.
Customer offers RM60 for 500 units. Spare capacity exists.
Relevant profit = (60 – 45) × 500 = RM7,500 positive → Accept
Make or Buy
Cost to make: DM 12 + DL 8 + Variable OH 5 + Allocated Fixed OH 6 = RM31
Supplier quote: RM28
Relevant make cost = 12+8+5 = RM25 (ignore allocated fixed if it continues)
Buying saves RM3 per unit → Buy if quality and reliability are acceptable.

5. Exam Tips

  • Always identify what is relevant – future and differential.
  • Draw contribution format income statements for CVP questions.
  • State assumptions of CVP (linear costs/revenues, constant sales mix, etc.).
  • For ABC, focus on identifying cost drivers.
  • Show clear workings; labels (DM, DL, OH) matter.
