Possible Assignment Ideas

Realistic tasks that lecturers commonly set for these core units. Useful for practice or anticipating assessment.

ACW1120 – Financial Accounting 1

  • Journal & Ledger Exercise: Record 12–15 transactions for a sole trader, post to T-accounts, prepare trial balance and basic financial statements.
  • Adjusting Entries Case: Given a list of year-end information (prepayments, accruals, depreciation), prepare all adjusting entries and the adjusted trial balance.
  • Conceptual Framework Essay: Explain the objective of general-purpose financial reporting and the qualitative characteristics. Apply them to a real Malaysian public-listed company.
  • Error Correction: Identify and correct a series of accounting errors and show the effect on profit and the balance sheet.

BFW1001 – Foundations of Finance

  • TVM Problem Set: Calculate PV, FV, annuity values and loan amortisation schedules for different compounding frequencies.
  • Capital Budgeting Mini-Project: Evaluate two mutually exclusive projects using NPV, IRR and payback. Recommend one with justification.
  • Financial System Report: Describe the role of Bank Negara Malaysia, Bursa Malaysia and major financial institutions in the Malaysian financial system.
  • Risk & Return Reflection: Compare the historical returns and volatility of two asset classes (e.g., KLCI vs government bonds) and discuss the risk-return trade-off.

BTW1042 – Business Law

  • Contract Problem Question: Analyse a multi-issue scenario involving offer/acceptance, consideration, misrepresentation and remedies using IRAC.
  • Business Structure Advice: Advise a client who wants to start a café with two partners on the advantages and disadvantages of partnership vs private limited company under Malaysian law.
  • Negligence Case Study: Determine whether a professional (accountant/auditor) owes a duty of care and whether that duty was breached.
  • Agency Scenario: Decide whether a principal is bound by a contract entered into by an agent with apparent authority.

ECM1953 – Principles of Economics

  • Market Analysis: Using demand-supply diagrams, analyse the effect of a subsidy or tax on a specific market (e.g., petrol, housing, e-hailing).
  • Market Structure Report: Choose a Malaysian industry and classify it (perfect competition / oligopoly / monopoly). Justify with evidence on number of firms, barriers and pricing behaviour.
  • Macro Policy Brief: Explain how Bank Negara’s interest rate decision or a government Budget measure is expected to affect AD, inflation and unemployment.
  • Elasticity Application: Collect price and quantity data for a product and estimate PED. Discuss pricing implications for the firm.

ETW1001 – Introduction to Statistical Analysis

  • Descriptive Statistics Report: Using a given dataset (sales, student scores, etc.), produce summary statistics, graphs and a short written interpretation in Excel.
  • Confidence Interval & Hypothesis Test: Construct a 95% CI for a mean and perform a one-sample or two-sample t-test. Interpret results in business language.
  • Simple Regression Project: Build a regression model relating advertising spend to sales (or similar). Report coefficients, R², residual analysis and business recommendations.
  • Dashboard Task: Create an Excel dashboard with key descriptive metrics and charts for a fictional company.

MGW1010 – Introduction to Management

  • Theory Critique Essay: Critically evaluate Scientific Management or Bureaucracy in the context of modern knowledge-work or remote teams.
  • Leadership Case Analysis: Analyse a real CEO’s leadership style using transformational / transactional / servant frameworks.
  • Ethics Dilemma: Apply ethical frameworks to a contemporary business ethics case (e.g., data privacy, greenwashing, labour practices).
  • Organisational Observation: Observe a real organisation (or use public information) and map its structure, culture and environmental influences.

ACW2220 – Management Accounting 1

  • CVP Analysis Project: For a given product, calculate break-even, margin of safety, and the sales needed for a target profit. Perform sensitivity analysis.
  • Relevant Costing Scenarios: Solve a series of short decision problems (special order, make-or-buy, keep-or-drop) and recommend actions with supporting calculations.
  • Job Costing Exercise: Prepare job cost sheets, allocate overhead, and determine cost per unit for several jobs.
  • Cost Behaviour Analysis: Using Excel, separate mixed costs into fixed and variable components (high-low or regression) and estimate total cost at different activity levels.
